Electronic signatures

Inkly is a tool to place electronic signatures on documents. Whether an electronic signature is valid for a particular document depends on the law that applies to it and on the parties. Electronic signatures are generally valid for everyday agreements in many countries, but some documents (for example wills, certain court and family-law documents, property deeds and notarised papers) may require other forms of signature.
